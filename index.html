Buyer Questions

Frequently asked questions about Moxman benefits.

Everything you need to know about group health insurance, onboarding, cashless claims, and coverage terms.

What is the minimum team size required to get group health insurance with Moxman?

Moxman supports Indian organisations of all stages, starting from teams with as few as 5 to 7 employees up to large enterprises with thousands of members across multiple office locations in India. We do not impose restrictive minimum headcount barriers on early-stage teams.

Can employees cover their family members and dependants?

Yes. You can configure plans covering employee-only (1E), employee with spouse and children (1E + 3D), or extended plans covering dependent parents and parents-in-law. Employees can also top up their sum insured or add family members during the digital onboarding window.

How does employee onboarding work once our policy is issued?

Onboarding is fully digital and paperless. HR uploads the employee census roster or connects their HRMS. Moxman automatically sends welcome invites to team members, who can immediately download their digital cashless health cards and manage dependants on their employee dashboard.

How do cashless claims work at partner network hospitals?

For planned or emergency hospital admissions at any of the 9,500+ network hospitals across India, the employee presents their digital Moxman health card at the hospital insurance desk. Our dedicated claims desk liaises directly with the hospital and TPA to issue pre-authorisation swiftly.

What happens if an employee receives treatment at a non-network hospital?

If an employee receives treatment at a non-network hospital, they can submit a digital reimbursement claim through the employee dashboard in under 3 minutes. Simply upload photos of the discharge summary, hospital bills, and payment receipts. Our claims desk tracks the file until reimbursement is credited.

How does Moxman determine pricing for our team's policy?

Pricing is calculated based on your total team size, average age demographics, chosen base sum insured (e.g. ₹3L, ₹5L, or ₹10L), family coverage configuration, and any selected add-ons such as outpatient OPD, maternity benefits, or corporate buffer funds.

Can we migrate our existing corporate policy to Moxman without losing waiting period credits?

Yes. Under IRDAI portability guidelines, your team retains complete continuity of coverage. Completed waiting periods for pre-existing diseases, maternity, and specific ailments carry over seamlessly to your new Moxman policy.

How does Moxman protect employee medical privacy and corporate data?

We treat employee healthcare data with strict confidentiality. Medical diagnoses, consultation records, and claim details are accessible only by authorized claims specialists and are never shared with employers or unauthorized third parties.

What level of support do our HR team and employees receive?

Every employer receives a dedicated Corporate Account Manager for policy administration, endorsements, and renewals. In addition, employees and their families have access to a 24/7 emergency claims escalation desk via phone and WhatsApp during hospitalisation.
